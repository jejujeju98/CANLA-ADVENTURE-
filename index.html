About CANLA ADVENTURE

CANLA ADVENTURE has been working in the tourism and guiding industry in North Sulawesi since around 2017. At that time there was no official name and no plan to create a travel brand like today.


Over the years, CANLA ADVENTURE has professionally handled many guests from Indonesia and from countries around the world. From wildlife photographers to families and adventure travelers, our team has experience creating safe and unforgettable journeys.


Although the name CANLA ADVENTURE was officially created in 2023, it is only now being publicly introduced as a professional local travel experience.

Travel Packages

Tangkoko Wildlife Tour

Discover Sulawesi Crested Black Macaque, Tarsius Spectral Gursky, Bear Cuscus, and Knobbed Hornbill.

Tangkoko Birding Tour

Special birdwatching experience focused on endemic birds from Tangkoko Nature Reserve.

Tangkoko Boat Tour Mangrove & Hot Spring

Mangrove exploration combined with relaxing hot spring experiences and coastal scenery.

Batuangus & Dolphin Seek Tour

Enjoy sunrise moments and search for dolphins around North Sulawesi coastline.

Tomohon & Tondano Highland Tour

Explore volcano landscapes, mountain atmosphere, lakes, flower gardens, and local culture.
